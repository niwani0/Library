Executive objective & case for change

What we are trying to achieve — and why current plans fall short

The ambition

50% wealth penetration is the governing ambition. Reaching it requires more than converting the existing portfolio.

Why the current trajectory falls short

  • ▸ Conversion of the on-book base alone cannot cover the gap.
  • ▸ We keep acquiring low-quality customers who never fund or qualify.
  • ▸ Penetration gains are partly cosmetic — driven by shrinking the denominator, not growing wealth.

Two non-negotiable outcomes

Outcome 1

Grow absolute wealth customers and NNIA. Real, retained net-new invested assets — not recycled money.

Outcome 2

Improve penetration without relying solely on denominator reduction. Clean-up supports health but cannot be the primary route to 50%.

We cannot convert our way out of continuously acquiring low-quality customers.

Portfolio dilution diagnostic · value protection

What is structurally dragging penetration and productivity down?

Sources of structural dilution

  • ▸ Customers below the qualification threshold
  • ▸ Grace-period customers
  • ▸ Persistently inactive Premier customers
  • ▸ Re-upgraded customers with no incremental value
  • ▸ P1PA / multi-market customers with unclear qualification
  • ▸ Low-value customers consuming RM capacity
  • ▸ Customers cycling between upgrade and downgrade
  • ▸ Markets improving penetration only by denominator reduction

A simple portfolio classification

Grow

Clear upside; invest.

Convert

Credible near-term path.

Retain

Valuable; protect.

Migrate

Right-fit service model.

Downgrade

Persistently unqualified.

Exit / inactive

No credible path.

Primary diagnostic question: Which customers have a credible path to value, and which are permanently diluting the portfolio?

NTB action plan

Make acquisition the first quality-control point

1 · Define "good NTB"

An account opened is not "good". A good NTB customer is:

  • funded within X days
  • TRB qualified within Y days
  • demonstrates wealth readiness
  • generates NNIA within Z days
  • stays qualified past early life

2 · Introduce quality gates

Apply the definition by:

  • market
  • channel
  • campaign
  • proposition
  • product hook
  • customer source

3 · Separate acquisition from conversion

Track distinctly:

  • acquired
  • funded
  • qualified
  • wealth activated
  • NNIA producing
  • sustained

4 · Reallocate acquisition investment

  • Scale high-quality channels
  • Redesign weak channels
  • Stop persistently dilutive acquisition

5 · Build hero conversion pathways

  • TD hook → income / yield solution
  • International relationship → offshore wealth activation
  • Mortgage relationship → investable balance conversion
  • Card-only customer → funded Premier & wealth activation

Portfolio clean-up action plan

Remove structural dilution — without gaming the denominator

A · Reaffirm eligibility

  • Qualification rules
  • Grace periods
  • International / multi-market treatment
  • Upgrade & downgrade rules

B · Apply disciplined actions

  • Retain high-potential customers
  • Migrate to appropriate service models
  • Downgrade persistently unqualified
  • Protect temporary but credible recovery paths

C · Prevent re-entry

  • Stop upgrade–downgrade recycling
  • Minimum quality thresholds on upgrades
  • Review origin & performance of upgraded customers
Guardrail — Clean-up supports portfolio health, but it cannot be the primary route to achieving 50%.
